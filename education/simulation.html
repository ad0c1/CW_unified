01 / QUESTION

Correlation tells us how strong an edge is. Not who made it strong.

Two edges can have the same pooled correlation and represent completely different realities.

Real minority structure

One group carries a whole community

The same 30% of respondents supports every edge inside block C. Their shared carrier profile is a mechanism.

Distractor structure

Different people create every edge

Each cross-block edge is supported by its own unrelated 10%. The pooled magnitude looks real; the carriers do not cohere.

same |ρ|  ≠  same mechanism  →  inspect the respondent × edge contribution

03 / ESTIMATOR

The simulation must confront the formula’s internal tension.

Minority support is concentrated. Yet the current Gini factor rewards diffuse support. The overlap term must rescue coherent minorities—or the product formula is wrong.

w* = |ρ| × (1 − Gini) × max(Ō, 0)

04 / IDENTIFICATION

One experiment would confound two possible failures. So we run two.

PRIMARY · ESTIMATOR LEVEL

Can the score tell mechanisms apart?

  • Keep every true C and distractor edge available.
  • Rank C edges above matched distractors.
  • Measure AUROC and score separation.
CO-PRIMARY · END TO END

Can the deployed pipeline recover C?

  • TMFG selects edges using raw |ρ| first.
  • CW reweights only surviving edges.
  • Walktrap recovers the partition—or not.
Why this split matters

If TMFG drops C, CW never gets a chance.

A successful estimator with failed end-to-end recovery means candidate-edge selection is the bottleneck. It does not falsify carrier coherence.

06 / VERDICTS

The result cannot be rescued by storytelling afterward.

Each possible outcome already has a meaning.

A

Full CW wins at both levels

Support the estimator and proceed to broader recovery and multiplicity simulations.

B

Overlap-only wins; full CW fails

The carrier-coherence idea survives, but the Gini product does not. Revise the formula in a new preregistered Simulation 1b.

C

Estimator wins; pipeline fails

Raw-correlation TMFG excluded the minority edges. Candidate-edge selection is the bottleneck.

D

Both levels fail

The central coherent-minority recovery claim is falsified in its intended setting.

1,000 confirmatory replicates · fixed seeds · no post-result tuning
The purpose of a simulation is not to make the method look good. It is to create a world where the method can be wrong.
